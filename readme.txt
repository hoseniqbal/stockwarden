Records every stock change with its reason, emails you when you oversell, sends one daily low stock summary and shows what will run out soon.

== Description ==

"Why does it say 3 when we had 20 yesterday?" WooCommerce changes stock for orders, refunds, edits and imports, but it never tells you why a number changed. Low stock emails arrive one product at a time, and you only notice a best seller is gone when it is already sold out.

StockWarden gives you the full story and an early warning.

= What it does =

* **Stock history.** Every change is recorded with before and after: sold in order #1234, returned from a cancelled order, changed by a staff member, or changed by an import or another plugin.
* **Oversold email.** Know at once when stock goes below zero, before you ship orders you cannot fill.
* **One daily summary.** Sold out, running out and low stock products in one email, instead of one email per product.
* **Running out forecast.** StockWarden learns how fast each product sells and shows how many days of stock are left, so you know what to order first.
* **Dashboard** with sold out, running out soon, low stock and tracked products.

= Privacy =

StockWarden stores product IDs, stock levels, order numbers and the staff member who made a change. No customer details. No external requests.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate StockWarden. WooCommerce must be active.
2. Make sure "Track stock quantity" is on for the products you want to watch.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it work with variations? =

Yes. Each variation with its own stock is tracked.

= Why is the forecast empty at first? =

It needs a few days of sales to learn how fast products sell. It gets better every day.

= Does it change my stock? =

Never. It only records and reports.
